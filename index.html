About

Hi, I’m André. I was born in Brasília and am currently based in São Paulo. When I am not studying or working with economics, I enjoy following sports, especially São Paulo FC and the San Francisco 49ers. I also like reading, playing the guitar, and playing video games, particularly The Witcher, my favorite game series. Over the years, I have worked as a Teaching Assistant for several courses, particularly in macroeconomics, at both the undergraduate and graduate levels.

I am a PhD student in Economic Theory at the University of São Paulo. My research focuses on macroeconomics, particularly the interactions between fiscal and monetary policy, inflation dynamics, DSGE models, and the Brazilian economy. In my master’s dissertation, I investigated the relationship between fiscal backing and inflation persistence in twenty-first-century Brazil using structural macroeconomic modeling and Bayesian methods.

During my PhD, I intend to expand this research agenda by incorporating open-economy and heterogeneous-agent models, while continuing to examine monetary-fiscal interactions as the broader framework of my work.
