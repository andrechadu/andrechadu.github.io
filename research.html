Research

My research interests lie in macroeconomics, particularly in the interactions between fiscal and monetary policy, inflation dynamics, and the Brazilian economy. I am also interested in DSGE models, Bayesian methods, and computational approaches to macroeconomic research.

Current research

PhD in Economic Theory

I began my PhD in Economic Theory at the University of São Paulo in August 2026, under the supervision of Mauro Rodrigues Júnior. My doctoral research focuses on macroeconomic questions involving fiscal and monetary policy, inflation dynamics, and open-economy models.
