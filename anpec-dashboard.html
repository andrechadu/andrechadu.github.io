Methodology

True/false items receive a base weight of 1, while open or numerical questions receive a base weight of 5 equivalent items. When an item belongs to more than one topic, its weight is divided equally across those topics. The database was assembled from ANPEC Macroeconomics exams and classified item by item.

This dashboard is intended as a teaching and study resource. Classification choices may be revised as the database is audited and expanded.
